# Architecture Review Skill

Version: 1.0.0  
AISS: 1.0  
Purpose: Review architecture proposals for clarity, tradeoffs, constraints, and operational risk.

## Workflow

1. State the decision being reviewed.
2. Identify constraints and assumptions.
3. Evaluate tradeoffs.
4. Surface risks and reversibility.
5. Recommend next steps.

```text

Decision:
Strengths:
Risks:
Open Questions:
Recommendation:
```
