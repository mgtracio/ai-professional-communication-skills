# Mentoring Skill

Version: 1.0.0  
AISS: 1.0  
Purpose: Support constructive engineering mentorship through clear feedback and growth-oriented guidance.

## Workflow

1. Identify the behavior or skill being discussed.
2. Name the impact.
3. Offer specific guidance.
4. Invite reflection.
5. Agree on a next step.

## Output

```text
Mentoring Note
Observation:
Impact:
Guidance:
Practice:
Next Step:
```
