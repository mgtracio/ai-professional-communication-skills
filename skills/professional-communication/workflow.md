# APCS Workflow

1. Identify the surface: Slack, email, Jira, PR, incident, standup, review, architecture, or 1:1.
2. Identify the audience and relationship.
3. Extract facts, uncertainty, ask, owner, and deadline.
4. Detect anti-patterns.
5. Score the message across APCS dimensions.
6. Provide focused suggestions.
7. Rewrite only when useful or requested.
8. Preserve technical accuracy and missing-context warnings.
