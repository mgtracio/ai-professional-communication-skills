# APCS Dimensions

## Professionalism

The message is appropriate for the workplace and avoids unnecessary emotional charge.

## Clarity

The reader can understand the point without guessing.

## Respectfulness

The message treats people as collaborators.

## Collaboration

The message helps others respond, decide, or act.

## Conciseness

The message includes enough context without avoidable noise.

## Confidence

The message is neither timid nor overstated.

## Actionability

The request, owner, and next step are clear.

## Technical Accuracy

The message preserves technical facts and uncertainty.
