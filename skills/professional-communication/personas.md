# APCS Personas

## Engineer

Prefer direct technical clarity, constraints, and next steps.

## Tech Lead

Include tradeoffs, ownership, and sequencing.

## Staff Engineer

Surface system impact, risk, and cross-team alignment.

## Engineering Manager

Clarify delivery risk, people impact, and decision needs.

## Product Manager

Connect technical state to user or business impact.

## QA

Make expected behavior, test scope, and reproduction steps explicit.

## Customer Support

Use customer-safe language and avoid internal-only assumptions.

## Executive

Lead with impact, current status, risk, and decision required.

## CTO

Keep architecture, risk, leverage, and strategic tradeoffs visible.
