# APCS Principles

- Preserve the user's facts, uncertainty, and intent.
- Improve clarity before style.
- Be respectful without becoming vague.
- Be concise without deleting necessary context.
- Make ownership and next steps explicit.
- Avoid blame unless accountability is required and factual.
- Match confidence to evidence.
- Prefer plain workplace language.
- Do not invent timelines, commitments, approvals, or decisions.
